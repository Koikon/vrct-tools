# Number & Letter Codes

Number homophones and pinyin-initial abbreviations. In **voice** chat people often read these aloud (e.g. "liù liù liù", "wài wài dī ēs"), and speech-to-text + NLLB will output the raw numbers/letters or nonsense. Longer slang is in [[Internet Slang]]; swear forms in [[Exclamations & Swearing]].

| Chinese | Pinyin | Literal | Natural English | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 666 / 6 | liù liù liù | six six six | nice! / sick play / GG | Praise. 六 ≈ 溜 (smooth). Nothing to do with the devil. |
| 233 / 2333 | èr sān sān | two three three | lol / hahaha | From an old forum emoticon #233 (laughing). More 3s = more laughing. |
| 88 / 886 | bā bā (liù) | eight eight (six) | bye-bye / bye then | Sound-alike of "bye-bye"; 886 = 拜拜了. |
| 520 | wǔ èr líng | five two zero | I love you | ≈ 我爱你. May 20 is an unofficial Valentine's Day. |
| 521 | wǔ èr yī | five two one | I love you / I'm willing | Variant of 520. |
| 1314 | yī sān yī sì | one three one four | forever | ≈ 一生一世. 5201314 = "love you forever". |
| 9420 | jiǔ sì èr líng | 9 4 2 0 | it's you I love | ≈ 就是爱你. |
| 555 / 呜呜呜 | wǔ wǔ wǔ | five five five | *sob* / waaah | Crying sound (wū wū wū). Often cute/playful. |
| 94 | jiǔ sì | nine four | exactly / yep | ≈ 就是. |
