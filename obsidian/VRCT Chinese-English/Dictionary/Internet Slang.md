# Internet Slang

Mainland internet slang (mostly Bilibili / Douyin / Weibo era) that machine translation tends to render literally. For pure number/letter codes see [[Number & Letter Codes]]; for swearing see [[Exclamations & Swearing]].

| Chinese | Pinyin | Literal | Natural English | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 666 / 6 | liù liù liù | six six six | nice! / sick! / GG, well played | Casual praise, from gaming; 六 sounds like 溜 "smooth/skilled". MT may output "six hundred sixty-six". See [[Number & Letter Codes]]. |
| yyds / 永远的神 | yǒngyuǎn de shén | eternal god | the GOAT / legendary | Casual, very common. Trad. 永遠的神. From esports chat. |
| 绝绝子 | jué jué zi | absolute-absolute-child | amazing / slay / 10/10 | Casual, slightly cringe/overused (Xiaohongshu style). Trad. 絕絕子. |
| 绝了 | jué le | extinct / absolute | incredible / unreal / that's peak | Can be positive (amazing) or sarcastic (unbelievable). MT: "it's extinct". Trad. 絕了. |
| 破防 | pò fáng | break defense | I'm wrecked / that got to me / hit me hard | From gaming (defense broken). Emotional: moved, hurt, or triggered. MT: "broke the defense". |
| 破大防 | pò dà fáng | break big defense | totally wrecked / fully triggered | Stronger 破防. |
| 笑死 / 笑死我了 | xiào sǐ (wǒ le) | laugh to death | lmao / I'm dying | Casual. MT: "laughed to death" (not literal death!). Abbr. xswl. |
| xswl | — | (笑死我了) | lmao | See [[Number & Letter Codes]]. |
| awsl / 啊我死了 | ā wǒ sǐ le | ah I died | I can't even / too cute, I'm dead | Overwhelmed by cuteness/emotion. MT: "I'm dead" (not a threat). |
| 蚌埠住了 | bèng bù zhù le | (Bengbu city) + can't hold | I can't hold it in / I'm losing it | Pun on 绷不住了 (can't keep a straight face / can't hold back tears). MT outputs "Bengbu" (a city). |
| 绷不住了 | bēng bu zhù le | can't stretch/hold | I'm cracking up / can't keep it together | Original form of the above. Trad. 繃不住了. |
| 摸鱼 | mō yú | touch fish | slacking off | Casual; esp. at work. MT: "touching fish". Trad. 摸魚. |
| 躺平 | tǎng píng | lie flat | checking out / not trying anymore | Rejecting rat-race pressure. |
| 摆烂 | bǎi làn | display rotten | giving up / not even trying | Stronger than 躺平; letting things go to hell. Trad. 擺爛. |
| 内卷 / 卷 | nèi juǎn | inner roll (involution) | rat race / over-competing / tryhard | 太卷了 = "it's so competitive". Trad. 內卷. |
| 社死 | shè sǐ | social death | so embarrassing / I want to die (of cringe) | Public humiliation moment. |
| 社牛 | shè niú | social ox | social butterfly / zero shame | Opposite of 社恐 (socially anxious). |
| 社恐 | shè kǒng | social fear | socially anxious / introvert | Common self-description in VR. |
| 好家伙 | hǎo jiāhuo | good guy/tool | whoa / holy cow / well, damn | Surprise or ironic amazement; from crosstalk comedy. MT: "good guy". Trad. 好傢伙. |
| 离谱 | lí pǔ | leave the score | ridiculous / wild / that's absurd | Very common. Trad. 離譜. |
| 无语 | wú yǔ | no words | speechless / I can't / ugh | Mild annoyance. Trad. 無語. |
| 芭比Q了 | bā bǐ Q le | Barbie Q | we're screwed / it's over | "Barbecue" pun; from a gaming streamer: 完了芭比Q了. |
| 栓Q | shuān Q | tie-Q | "thank you" (sarcastic) / thanks, I hate it | From a Guilin farmer's viral accented "thank you". Ironic. |
| 真香 | zhēn xiāng | really fragrant | okay it's actually good / I take it back | Swore you wouldn't, then love it. MT: "really smells good". |
| 凡尔赛 | fán'ěrsài | Versailles | humblebragging | Trad. 凡爾賽. MT: "Versailles". |
| 打call | dǎ call | hit call | hype up / cheer for / big support | From idol fandom glow-stick cheering. |
| 上头 | shàng tóu | go to head | hooked / obsessed / can't stop | Also "tipsy" in drinking context. |
| 下头 | xià tóu | go down head | a turn-off / ick / cringe | Opposite of 上头. Trad. 下頭. |
| emo / emo了 | ēi-mó | (English emo) | feeling down / sad | Mood, not the music genre. |
| 麻了 | má le | numb | I'm done / dead inside / speechless | Exasperation; MT: "numb" / "hemp". |
| 裂开 | liè kāi | split open | I'm losing it / I'm broken | Frustration/despair. Trad. 裂開. |
| 啊这 | ā zhè | ah this | uhh... / welp / awkward | Awkward/speechless reaction. Trad. 啊這. |
| 寄 / 寄了 | jì (le) | mailed | it's over / we're dead / GG | Pun on 死 (die) via 寄 = post/mail. MT: "sent/mailed". |
| 凉凉 / 凉了 | liáng liáng | cold cold | done for / it's over | Trad. 涼涼. |
| 吃瓜 | chī guā | eat melon | watching the drama / grabbing popcorn | 吃瓜群众 = bystanders enjoying drama. |
| 杠精 | gàng jīng | bar spirit | person who argues everything / contrarian troll | Mildly insulting. Trad. 槓精. |
| 柠檬精 / 酸了 | níngméng jīng / suān le | lemon spirit / sour | jealous (jokingly) | Trad. 檸檬精. |
| 佛系 | fó xì | Buddha-style | chill / go-with-the-flow | |
| 舔狗 | tiǎn gǒu | licking dog | simp | Derogatory-ish. |
| 打工人 | dǎgōng rén | work-for-hire person | wage slave (self-mocking) | Trad. 打工人. |
| 干饭 | gàn fàn | do rice | time to eat! / chow down | Casual, cheerful. Trad. 幹飯. |
| 冲鸭 | chōng yā | charge duck | let's go! | Cute pun on 冲呀. Trad. 衝鴨. |
| 奥利给 | ào lì gěi | (sound) | let's gooo! / you got this | Meme from a Kuaishou streamer. Trad. 奧利給. |
| 硬核 | yìng hé | hard core | hardcore / intense / legit | |
| 翻车 | fān chē | car flips | epic fail / it backfired | Trad. 翻車. |
| 典 | diǎn | classic | classic (mocking) / so typical | Mocking a cringe/typical take. |
