# Exclamations & Swearing

Reactions and profanity, each with a **severity**: *mild* (fine anywhere), *rude* (crude but everyday among friends), *offensive* (genuine insult / slur-level). Many online forms are euphemistic homophones of 肏 (cào, "fuck"). Slang reactions like 笑死 / 绝了 are in [[Internet Slang]].

## Severity key
- **Mild** — ok in any casual chat.
- **Rude** — vulgar; normal among friends, don't say to strangers.
- **Offensive** — real insult; if someone says it *to* you it's hostile.
